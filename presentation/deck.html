Purpose. Show how I approached an ambiguous dataset before showing any result: every number that follows is only as good as the decisions that made the comparisons fair.

Say. I started from the raw captures exactly as returned. I then checked coverage — whether each scheduled collection returned the expected locations at the expected hourly cadence. Next I resolved location identity, so that each feed maps to one approved canonical location. Only then did I match identical offers, and only matched or eligible evidence went into the price and change analysis.

Why exact matching. A matched pair shares the city, the collection event (the same collection job), the rental period, the exact vehicle identity — name, type, transmission, seats and bags — the currency and the price basis, with one airport and one downtown offer. I avoided similarity matching by vehicle class or near-identical names because it would mix product differences into the price gap. That gives {{matched_pairs}} pairs from {{candidate_groups}} candidate groups, a match rate of {{match_rate}}; unmatched or ambiguous groups are excluded rather than guessed.

Why exclude rather than impute. One Calgary parent capture was incomplete, so its {{governed_excluded_captures}} location-captures are excluded from pricing instead of being filled in. Imputing would manufacture prices and changes that were never observed. The exclusion stays in the raw and audit populations, so it is counted and traceable even though it does not enter a pricing conclusion.

Observation versus explanation. When several products at one location move together, I report an investigation candidate, not proof of repricing, supplier behaviour or a collection failure; telling those apart needs collection logs or source snapshots. Likewise, visible assortment is what the collection returned, not actual supplier availability. Hourly observations of the same products are repeated measurements, so the results are descriptive, not independent causal evidence. And with one supplier and one apparent source mode, nothing here speaks to competitors or channels.
